Privacy and credits

DRAFT for Chris to check before launch. Far Shore Works makes Topple.

What we keep

  • On your device only: today's throws, your streak, which objects you have collected, and whether sound is on. This stays in your browser's local storage. Clearing your browser data erases it.
  • Nothing is sent to a server right now. There are no accounts, no sign-in and no ads in this version.
  • When ranks are switched on: we will store a random device id (not your name or email), the date, your three throws and your score, so we can tell you what share of players you beat. We will update this page first.
  • We do not use cookies and do not sell data.

Sharing

"Copy result" and "Challenge a friend" copy text to your clipboard. Nothing is posted for you.
